Practical guidance for families

Choosing home staff is easier when the requirement is written down before the first call. Note the locality, home or workplace setting, number of people involved, preferred days and hours, start date and the tasks that must be completed. This gives the agency enough context to discuss availability without making assumptions about your needs.

How to write a maid brief

List cleaning, laundry, utensils, cooking or childcare duties separately. State whether the role is part-time, full-time or live-in, and ask how salary, leave, replacement and agency charges are handled.

Questions for childcare support

Explain the child?s routine, supervision needs, school timings and emergency contacts. Ask about identity documents and references. A babysitter or domestic helper should not be treated as a nurse unless relevant qualifications are verified.

Patient-care planning

Describe the care setting, shift, mobility assistance and daily routine. Confirm the difference between an attendant and a qualified nurse, and keep medication, procedures and clinical decisions with the appropriate health professional.

Before confirming any arrangement

Ask for the duties, schedule, fees, payment terms and applicable policies in writing. Verify documents and references yourself, and confirm the person?s availability for your exact locality.
